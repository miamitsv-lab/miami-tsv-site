CFO VIDEOKE RESPONSIVE HTML PACKAGES
===================================

Included packages:

1) cfo-videoke-responsive-contain
   - Best when you want the full poster always visible.
   - Safest choice for preserving all top and bottom text.
   - May show more empty black space on some screens.

2) cfo-videoke-responsive-cover
   - Best when you want the design to fully fill the screen.
   - Works like a hero background.
   - Important text or edges may be cropped on some devices.

3) cfo-videoke-responsive-balanced
   - Recommended middle-ground version.
   - Keeps the full poster visible while filling more of the screen.
   - Adds light padding so the poster feels fuller without cropping.

Recommendation:
Use cfo-videoke-responsive-balanced if you want the best overall presentation.
Use cfo-videoke-responsive-contain if preserving every part of the poster is the top priority.
Use cfo-videoke-responsive-cover only if you prefer a full-bleed background look.
