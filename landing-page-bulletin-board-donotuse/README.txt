ANNIVERSARY CAROUSEL SITE
=========================

Open or upload index.html together with all included files and folders.
Do not separate index.html from carousel.css, carousel.js, or the slides folder.

Automatic display times:
- 112th Anniversary Thanksgiving: 60 seconds
- Miami 40th Anniversary countdown: 15 seconds

Navigation:
- Two circle dots appear at the bottom center.
- Clicking a dot immediately opens that page and restarts its full display time.
- Left and right arrow keys also change pages.

To change the timing later, edit SLIDE_DURATIONS near the top of carousel.js.
Values are milliseconds:
- 60_000 = 60 seconds
- 15_000 = 15 seconds
