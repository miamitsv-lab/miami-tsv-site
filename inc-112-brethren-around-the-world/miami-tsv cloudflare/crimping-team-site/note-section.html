Crimping RJ45 Ethernet Cables

A Practical Guide for TSV

TermMeaning / Note
Cable tester shotThe final proof that the crimped cable works. The tester lights must remain visible long enough for viewers to understand the result.
CalloutsSimple arrows, boxes, circles, or short notes that point to an important wire, tool, connector detail, action, or warning.
Close-up shotA tight camera view showing the cable, wires, RJ45 connector, crimping tool, or tester clearly.
Closing cardThe final branded screen after the cable-tester result. It reinforces “Reliable connection. Better worship experience.” and keeps TSV branding consistent.
Full rough cutA viewable beginning-to-end version using the uploaded recordings and completed voice-over. It may still contain minor caption, callout, audio, or timing refinements. Due August 2.
LabelsShort text overlays that identify tools, wire colors, steps, warnings, or actions.
Lower thirdA small graphic near the bottom third of the screen used for a speaker, topic, step, or tool label.
Pickup-shot ruleRecord a pickup only when the uploaded footage cannot clearly or accurately show T568B order, RJ45 insertion, crimping action, second-end repeat, or cable-tester proof.
Post-lock change ruleAfter pre-production lock, allow only accuracy fixes, readability fixes, audio cleanup, missing visuals, essential pickups, and small timing improvements.
Pre-production lockThe project is closed for major format, script, visual-style, and scene changes. Current work is post-production and necessary correction only.
RetakesExtra recordings used only to replace an unclear, incorrect, damaged, or missing technical shot.
Rough-cut review checklistCheck technical accuracy, complete scene order, readable labels, clean voice-over, visible critical close-ups, understandable pacing, and a short controlled fix list.
Scene-fitting cutsTrim each recording so it fits the planned scene and narration without removing critical technical detail.
Section cardsShort branded screens that introduce each major tutorial step and match the title-card and lower-third design.
Shot listThe planned list of camera views needed for the tutorial.
Simple production rulePrioritize clear close-up shots, correct wire order, clean audio, and readable labels over fancy effects.
Step cardsSimple branded screens or overlays that identify each major tutorial step.
T568B wire orderThe wiring standard that must be shown clearly and accurately throughout the tutorial.
Title cardThe opening branded screen that introduces the Crimping RJ45 Ethernet Cables tutorial.
Voice-over synchronizationAlign narration with the related tutorial footage so each instruction is heard while the matching step is clearly visible.
